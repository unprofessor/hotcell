## Goal

Hold work that is real but not scoped to a feature epic: design spikes,
research, investigations, and cross-cutting chores. Tickets here exist so
that work in flight is visible and can gate feature work, not because it
belongs to a shipping increment.

## Scope

- Design and research spikes — story `design-and-research`.

## Out of scope

- Anything with a natural feature home. This epic is the exception, not the
  default; prefer a feature epic whenever one fits.
